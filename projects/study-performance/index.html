THE CHALLENGE

Compare course signals together

Grades, attendance, and time spent studying are useful together, but separate course records are hard to compare at a glance.

THE APPROACH

Turn course data into a snapshot

I built a CSV-driven dashboard that calculates course averages and shows grade and study-hour comparisons alongside a detailed course table.

KEY DECISION

Use fictional, local sample data

The example avoids real student records. Imports stay in the browser, and invalid files are rejected without replacing the current dashboard.
