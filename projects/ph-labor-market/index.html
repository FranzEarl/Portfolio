PROJECT / PUBLIC LABOR STATISTICS

Philippines Labor Market Explorer

Explore annual employment and unemployment indicators from the World Bank. The data loads directly from its public API; no account or API key is needed.

THE SOURCE

Public country-level data

Indicators come from the World Bank's World Development Indicators API and can be refreshed any time.

THE METHOD

Annual modeled estimates

Several measures are ILO modeled estimates, not monthly vacancy counts or a direct measure of hiring.

THE LIMIT

Not skills demand

These indicators describe broad labor-market patterns and do not show which skills employers request.
